How to play

Fit the falling pieces together. Fill a horizontal line to clear it; clear four at once for more points. The outlined piece shows where yours will land. Every 10 lines, the pace picks up. The game ends when there's no room for the next piece.

Use ↑ or X to rotate clockwise, Z to rotate the other way, and P or Esc to pause. On a touch screen, use the buttons below the board. Your best score stays in this browser when storage is available.
